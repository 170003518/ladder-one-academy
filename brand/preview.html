Logo

Navy shield, three-rung ladder, Star of Life above. The rails run from a narrow gap at the top out to a wide one at the bottom, so the space they enclose above the top rung reads as an A. Rungs are colored bottom to top — star white EMT, ember Fire, line blue Paramedic — and in the app each one lights as its tier unlocks, which makes the logo the progress indicator.

Printed cards — 3×5 in, four up on Letter

Two 3 in columns with a 0.5 in gutter, two 5 in rows, inside a 0.5 in page margin. Each card carries the stamp top-left, the tier bar down the left edge, the card type top-right, and the footer line across the bottom. Dashed trim boxes and corner ticks are the cut guides.

Duplex: print the fronts sheet, put the stack back in unturned, then print the backs sheet. The backs grid is mirrored left-to-right so a long-edge flip lands each back on its own front.

Sheet 1 — fronts

L1A Concept

Non-rebreather mask

The highest-concentration oxygen an EMT gives a patient who is still breathing on their own.

High-yield
  • 10–15 L/min
  • Delivers ~90% O₂
  • Reservoir must be inflated before applying

Why it matters: the wrong device on a patient who is breathing poorly wastes the only intervention you have.

L1A Concept

Negligence: the four elements

All four must be present. Miss one and the claim fails.

  • Duty to act
  • Breach of that duty
  • Causation — the breach caused the harm
  • Damages — actual harm resulted

Why it matters: abandonment and refusal questions are really negligence questions wearing a different hat.

What to check

  • Does the A read in the logo at 32px and 16px, or do the rails close up?
  • Is the EMT bar visible against white paper, or too faint to see?
  • Toggle Black & white proof above — can you still tell the three tiers apart by pattern alone?
  • Print to PDF at 100% scale (not "fit to page") and measure a card. It should be exactly 3 × 5 in.
  • Fold the printed fronts sheet along the gutter — do the backs land where you expect?
